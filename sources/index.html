Our news sources 📰

Every NewsToons episode retells real reporting in words and pictures a child can follow. Each edition draws on its own country's news, and every fact is checked against the original report before it is drawn.

We name our sources in text, and never display their logos or brand marks: those belong to the newsrooms that earned them. NewsToons is an independent family project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any of the organisations listed here.

Each episode also keeps a public record of exactly which articles it drew on — see the sources.md file beside every episode in our archive.

🤖 Art and voices are AI-generated. Stories are written by people and AI together, and every fact is checked against real news reports. Inaccuracies may slip through diligent checks — if something looks wrong, tell a grown-up and check the news source.
