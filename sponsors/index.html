NewsToons

Today's news, tomorrow's adventure ✏️✨

For kids of all ages

Sponsorship Welcome 🤝

NewsToons is a small family project that turns each day's news into a friendly comic for children — free to read, in a growing number of countries and languages. We'd love help to keep it growing.

📺 News organisations

Every edition retells the day's reporting in kid-sized words and pictures, and always names where the facts came from. If you are a broadcaster or newsroom — in your own language or in English — and you'd like your name and logo shown on the editions that draw on your reporting, we would be glad to display them as a mark of participation. We never display a broadcaster's logo without written permission; today we credit our sources in text only.

🤖 AI companies

NewsToons is made by humans working with AI: research, scripts, artwork, voices and review. Credits and tokens are what keep the presses running, and sponsors who provide them are listed — with their logos, gladly — on every page. Bloome and MuleRun do exactly this today, and we're happy to talk to anyone who'd like to join them.

💛 Everyone else

Readers, parents, teachers, libraries: donations of any size help pay for artwork, voices and hosting. We'll add a donation link here shortly — until then, do just write to us.

What sponsorship does and doesn't buy

  • Sponsors are credited, never editorial. No sponsor chooses stories, reviews scripts, or appears inside an episode.
  • No advertising is ever shown to children — credits only, in the footer and on this page, in the manner of public broadcasting.
  • No reader data is sold or shared. We count visits anonymously and store nothing about any individual, child or adult.

Write to us through our GitHub page — we read everything.
